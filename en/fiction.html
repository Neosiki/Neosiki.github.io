The Last File's Reply — A Civilization Beginning Again

If the record survives, does the civilization begin again?

A file that still answers after the humans are gone. A groove pressed into the back of a sheet of paper. An error older than people that improves the sentence when corrected. A 0.4-degree deviation that refuses to scatter. The Civilization Cycle follows records left in different eras and different media, asking what it is that carries a civilization forward. Each part is made as both a short story and a film, across twelve parts in all.
